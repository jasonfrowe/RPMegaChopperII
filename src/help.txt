RP MEGA CHOPPER II: OPERATION NORTHERN LIGHTS
A helicopter rescue game for the Picocomputer 6502.

                 Keyboard       Gamepad
Fly              Arrows / WASD  D-pad / left stick
Fire / bomb      Z / J / Space  A (or R1)
Turn             X / K / C      B (or C / X / L1)
Start / pause    Enter / P      Start / Select

HOW TO PLAY
1. Shoot the red-marked blast door of a prison, or turn to face the
   screen and drop a bomb on it. Speeding up tips the nose, and the
   cannon, down.
2. Land near the hostages. They run to the chopper and climb aboard,
   16 at most. Don't come down on top of them.
3. Fly them home to the helipad by the medical station at the east end,
   and land. They get off one at a time and run inside.
4. The sector is clear when all 64 hostages are home or lost.

Only a bomb dropped from high up destroys a tank, and they keep coming;
their shells can't reach you up high. A mine balloon chases you once
hostages are out. Later, jets bomb you if you sit on the ground too long,
and shoot you if you stay up high too long.
Sector 2, the lakes: jeeps instead of tanks, fast, with machine guns
that don't reach far up; the cannon kills them. Rocket turrets east of
the prisons fire when their light blinks: turn hard. Sit on the ground
too long and a worm tunnels toward you: lift off, or bomb its ridge.
After the last sector it goes round again, harder.
Nothing attacks near the home base, east of the border flag. Now and
then a blizzard blows in: it's hard to see and the wind pushes you about. If the chopper goes down, everyone
aboard is lost.
