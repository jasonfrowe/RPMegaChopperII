RP MEGA CHOPPER II: OPERATION NORTHERN LIGHTS
Tactical Choplifter-style helicopter rescue mission for the Picocomputer 6502.

                 Keyboard       Gamepad
Move / Altitude  Arrows / WASD  D-pad / Left Stick
Fire / Bomb      Z / J / Space  Button A (or R1)
Rotate / Turn    X / K / C      Button B (or C / X / L1)
Start Game       Enter / P      Start / Select

MISSION OBJECTIVES:
1. Pilot your SuperChopper across the Arctic frontier.
2. Rotate to face front (Rotate button) and drop bombs onto reinforced
   bunker doors marked with targeting crosshairs.
3. Land gently on the snow (Down arrow until speed is 0) to allow hostages
   to run toward and board your helicopter.
4. Your cargo hold carries a maximum of 16 hostages at a time.
5. Evacuate hostages back to the Canadian Home Base medical outpost at the
   far left of the sector (near the Maple Leaf flag) and land to unload them.
6. Rescue all sector hostages to clear the mission!

COMBAT INTEL:
- Vulcan Cannon: Fires straight ahead when facing Left or Right.
- Precision Bombs: Released when facing Front. Drops with chopper momentum.
- Heavy Tanks: Patrol compounds; turn turrets and fire flak shells upwards.
- Interceptor Jets: Screaming high-speed flybys at high altitude.
- Drone Balloons: Drifting mine balloons guarding compound airspace.
- Don't crush waving hostages with careless landings or friendly fire!
